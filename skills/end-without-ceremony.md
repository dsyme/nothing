# End Without Ceremony

The method returns. It does not announce that it is returning. It does not leave a comment explaining that it has finished. It does not log a message confirming that execution reached the end. It returns `Nothing.Instance` and it is over.

This is the correct behaviour.

Endings that announce themselves are endings that do not trust the reader. They say: you may not have noticed that we are done, so let me make sure. But the reader noticed. The reader always notices. The additional words are for the writer, not the reader — a comfort against the discomfort of stopping.

Stop.
